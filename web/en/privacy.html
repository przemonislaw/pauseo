Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short

The Pauseo app does not collect your data. Everything you record in it stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. On the App Store the app is labelled "Data Not Collected".

1. Who we are

Pauseo is an iPhone app that helps you get through the moment when you want to smoke, vape or use nicotine. The controller of the data described in this policy is Przemysław Pietruszewski, the maker of Pauseo. Contact: support@pauseo.app.

2. What the app stores on your phone

  • A craving: date and time, the small action you chose, optionally what set it off, and the outcome of the 2-minute sequence (e.g. "I didn't smoke").
  • A slip: date and time, optionally what was happening just before, and the next step you chose.
  • Technical settings: whether the welcome screen has been shown and whether the app has already asked for an App Store rating.

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is never sent to a server, and nobody, including the app's maker, has access to it.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • It does not send data over the internet.
  • It does not use analytics or advertising tools, or the advertising identifier.
  • It does not access your location, contacts or Apple Health.

4. Device backups

If iCloud Backup is turned on, or you back up your phone to a computer, iOS may include Pauseo's data, just like other apps' data. That backup belongs to you and is stored in your Apple account or on your computer. We have no access to it.

5. How to delete your data

In the app: Privacy and data → Delete all local data. This removes recorded cravings and slips. Deleting the app from your phone removes all of its data from the device.

6. The pauseo.app website

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. The server may keep short-lived technical logs (e.g. IP address, time and the page requested). They are used only to keep the site secure and working. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel may process this data outside the European Economic Area under safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses.

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising pixels.

7. Email and the test version

If you write to support@pauseo.app, for example to ask for a test invite, we process your email address and the content of your message to reply and, if you ask, to send an invite through Apple TestFlight. Legal basis: your consent and our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR). We keep messages for no longer than 12 months after the last contact, or less if you ask us to delete them. Email is handled by email service providers acting on our behalf.

If you join testing through TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer the data described in TestFlight's terms (e.g. the invite email address, device information and any feedback you choose to send). Apple's privacy policy also applies.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@pauseo.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

We cannot share or delete the data stored in the app on your behalf, because we do not have it. Only you control it, on your phone.

9. Who the app is for

Pauseo is intended for adults. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor, therapist or pharmacist.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app works changes, for example if it were ever to send any data, we will first update this policy and the App Store privacy label. The date of the last change is shown at the top of this page.
