What to do when you want to smoke

By the creator of Pauseo · Updated 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

The urge to smoke or vape comes in waves. Here are a few simple things you can do in the next few minutes. They need no preparation or strong willpower, and several are exactly what the Pauseo app does in its 2-minute sequence.

How long does a nicotine craving last?

A nicotine craving often lasts a few minutes, although it varies from person to person (NHS). After cutting down on nicotine it can also show up at different times and with different intensity (National Cancer Institute). It is a common reaction of the body, not a sign of weakness.

7 steps for those few minutes

  1. Rate the urge from 1 to 10. Remember the number. Check again in a few minutes. Just noticing that the urge changes helps you see it doesn't stay at one level.
  2. Change your body position. Stand up, sit differently or take a few steps. Changing the setting is often an easier move than arguing with a thought.
  3. Sip water slowly. Focus on the drinking itself: the temperature, the taste, the movement of your hand.
  4. Relax your hands and shoulders. We often don't notice the body tensing during an urge. See if anything changes.
  5. Notice where you feel the urge. In your hands, throat, chest? Naming the place turns "I must" into something you can observe.
  6. Postpone the decision by a few minutes. You don't have to decide about the whole day, only the next few dozen seconds. Postponing is already a concrete step, even if you smoke later.
  7. Rate the urge again. Compare with the number from step 1. If it is higher, that's fine. Start from step 2 or come back in a moment.

What happens in the body after quitting

This is not an assessment of your health. It shows the general course and can be a helpful perspective.

  • Heart rate and blood pressure begin to drop within about 20 minutes of stopping smoking (WHO).
  • After about 8 hours without a cigarette, the level of carbon monoxide in the blood falls by half (NHS).
  • After about 2 days, taste and smell start to improve (NHS).

What if you smoke anyway?

That was a slip, not a failure. Many people need several attempts before quitting sticks, and another attempt doesn't mean starting from zero. Note what came before the urge (coffee, stress, company), because that is the most useful information for next time.

When to talk to a professional

This page is general education and support for getting through an urge. It is not medical advice. For medication, nicotine replacement therapy or strong withdrawal symptoms, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
