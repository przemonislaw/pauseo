Support

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to support@pauseo.app. I usually reply within a few days.

Do I need an account?

No. Pauseo works right after you install it, with no sign-up or login.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. The app sends nothing. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Privacy and data → Delete all local data. You can also delete the app from your phone.

I moved to a new phone and my data is gone

Pauseo has no account or sync, so data moves to a new phone only with your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). The app works the same without earlier data.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pauseo supports building habits and noticing what triggers the urge to smoke. It does not replace advice from a doctor, therapist or pharmacist. If you are considering medication or nicotine replacement therapy, or have strong withdrawal symptoms, talk to a professional.

Does Pauseo cost anything?

No. Support in the moment of a craving is and will stay free. No account, no ads.

If things feel very hard

If you are in immediate danger or might hurt yourself, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU) or reach out to someone you trust. Pauseo is not a crisis tool.
