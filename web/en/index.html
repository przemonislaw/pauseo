Support when a craving hits.

When the urge to smoke or vape comes, Pauseo walks you through the next few minutes: one small action, a breath, short prompts.

  • One tap, not a day counter.Open the app in the hard moment and know right away what to do.
  • A slip is information, not a reset.Nothing is lost and nobody judges you. You just note what set it off.
  • No account. Your data stays on your phone.The app sends nothing: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
