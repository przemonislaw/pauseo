Frequently asked questions

What is Pauseo?

Pauseo is a free iPhone app for people quitting or cutting down on smoking, vaping or nicotine. It helps in the exact moment of a craving: one small action and 2 minutes of short prompts that help you wait the urge out.

Is Pauseo free?

Yes. Help in the moment of a craving is and will stay free. The app has no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Pauseo needs no account and sends nothing to a server. You can delete your data in the app at any time.

What if I smoke anyway?

A slip resets nothing. Pauseo doesn't count days or streaks. You just note what set it off and come back to one small decision now.

Does Pauseo replace a doctor or nicotine replacement therapy?

No. Pauseo supports habits and getting through urges, but it is not medical advice and does not suggest medication or doses. For medication or strong withdrawal symptoms, talk to a qualified professional.

When will Pauseo be on the App Store, and on which phones?

Launch on the App Store is planned for December 2026. The app is for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and works in Polish and English.
